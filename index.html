The carts

Book one or put them together. Nadine brings everything, sets up at your home or small business, hosts it on the day and packs away after.

Cake cart

Your guests design their own mini cake. They choose a vanilla, chocolate or red velvet base, a buttercream, chocolate, vanilla or cream cheese frosting, add sprinkles and sparkles, and finish it with a chocolate, caramel, white chocolate or strawberry drizzle.

Charcuterie cart

A hosted cart with a variety of eating experiences, served with non-alcoholic drinks. Charcuterie boxes and cups can also be delivered, pre-packed, to a location of your choice.

Mini dessert bar

Trays of mini cakes and sweet treats to round off the charcuterie, or to stand on their own.

  • Set up where you are. At your home or at your small business.

  • Delivered options. Pre-packed treats, delivered to a location of your choice.
