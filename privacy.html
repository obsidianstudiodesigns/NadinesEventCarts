Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Nadine's Event Carts ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

The owner of the business is our Information Officer under POPIA. Contact them on the number above about anything in this policy.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • the date, venue or suburb and number of guests for your event
  • your order, colour scheme and any dietary or allergy information you share with us
  • a delivery address when we deliver boxes or cups to you
  • payment and invoice details when you book

How the forms on this website work

The booking forms (including the charcuterie cart booking form and the mini cake order form) and the "Send this cake to Nadine" button do not store or send anything to us by themselves. They open WhatsApp on your phone or computer with a message already written. Nothing reaches us unless you send that message yourself.

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry, check availability and give you a quote
  • to prepare your order, set up at your event and deliver to you
  • to take allergies and dietary needs into account when you tell us about them
  • to send invoices and keep records we are required to keep by law

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not send you marketing unless you ask for it.

Health information

If you tell us about an allergy or dietary need, we use it only to prepare your order safely and keep it no longer than we need it for that order.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to in order to fill your order or when the law requires it. The services we use to talk to you, such as WhatsApp and Facebook (Meta), process messages under their own privacy policies.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. To load, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • the company that hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the 3D graphics libraries

Links to Facebook, WhatsApp and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Photos

Photos on this website come from our Facebook page and show our own carts and treats. If a photo shows you or your event and you would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as we need them to deal with your request. Records of orders and invoices are kept for as long as tax and other laws require, usually five years, and then deleted.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us on the number above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Bookings are made by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
