# Nadine's Event Carts website

A single-page site for Nadine's Event Carts (Nadine's Cake Cart and Charcuterie Cart, East Rand), with privacy and terms pages.

- `index.html` is the website; `privacy.html` and `terms.html` are the legal pages.
- `js/scene.js` is the 3D scene: Nadine's white panelled cart with its pine top, umbrella, foil trays of mini cakes,
  topping bowls, drizzle bottles and the "create your own" chalkboard, in a real Gauteng garden. Scrolling moves the
  camera from the cart (hero) down to a close-up of the cake you design.
- `js/cake.js` builds the mini cakes: foil tray, sponge, piped star rosettes, toppings and drizzle. Changes animate in
  (sprinkles fall, the drizzle pours). The cake you design starts plain, with no sprinkles or drizzle.
- `js/menu.js` is the cake cart menu from Nadine's posters (bases, frostings, colours, toppings, drizzles).
- `js/main.js` connects scrolling, the cake builder and the booking form.

## Preview locally
The 3D scene uses JavaScript modules, so the site has to be served rather than opened as a file:

    python -m http.server 5540

Then open http://localhost:5540. Add `#debug` to the URL to expose `window.__cart` in the console.

## Content sources
- Logo: `logo.jpg` in this folder, cut out and straightened into `assets/brand/logo-round*.png`.
- Photos in `assets/photos/` come from the business's Facebook page (facebook.com/profile.php?id=61550542030775).
  The page posts flyers, so the real photos are cropped out of them; the originals are in `tools/src/`.
- Contact details, service areas and the menu come from the Facebook "About" section and the flyers.
  The page has no email address, so the site uses phone and WhatsApp only.
- There are no reviews on the site because the Facebook page has none yet (0 reviews).
- No prices are shown because none are published. Add them once Nadine confirms.

## 3D assets
`assets/3d/` holds CC0 assets from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com): the "Pretoria Gardens" panorama and HDRI, and the
"Coated Pine" and "White Planks Clean" textures. The foil, sponge and frosting normal maps are generated by
`python tools/make-assets.py` (needs Pillow, numpy and opencv-python), which also rebuilds the photo crops, logo,
favicons, app icons and `assets/brand/og-image.jpg`.

`assets/3d/hero-poster.jpg` is a still of the hero shot. It shows while the 3D loads and on devices without WebGL.
Retake it if the hero camera changes.

Performance: the scene only redraws when something changes (scroll, pointer, cake changes). Fast GPUs also get a
slow hand-held camera drift. Weaker GPUs (Intel, phones) get half-resolution ambient occlusion, and the render
resolution drops if frames are slow.

## Hosting and domain
Canonical links, the link preview, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` point at
`https://obsidianstudiodesigns.github.io/NadinesEventCarts/`, matching the other Obsidian Studio sites on GitHub Pages.
If the site is hosted somewhere else or the client gets their own domain, replace that address in `index.html`,
`privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` (and add a `CNAME` file for a custom domain on Pages).
